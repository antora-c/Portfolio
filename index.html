About

I work where design meets the backlog.

I start with the unglamorous part of a problem: watching how people actually make decisions. Then I work with PMs and engineers to turn that into a direction and ship it. Most of my work is dense, rule-driven, data-heavy territory, where clarity and accuracy matter more than polish.

Design

Information architecture, interaction and design systems in Figma, plus working React, Tailwind and shadcn prototypes tested against real SQL and API data.

Product ownership

PRDs, my own Jira backlog, and structured reviews with engineers, PMs and clinical operations leads across US and India time zones.

Research and data

Operator interviews and usability testing, then SQL when an argument needs evidence, not opinion.

AI in the workflow

Claude with a persistent product context file, Figma Make, Lovable and Figma MCP for research synthesis, prototypes and specs. I direct and review its output critically.

Also

The part that does not show up in Figma.

Backlog management

I run my own Jira backlog: 20 to 30 actionable tickets across Highest, High and Medium, sequenced against real engineering dates and grouped by component so parallel work does not collide. I also run structured reviews with engineers, PMs and clinical operations leads across US and India time zones.

Stakeholder facilitation

On a consolidated invoicing request, I turned one line from a client into a scoped v1: added the contracted-rate override they raised later, deferred payment automation on purpose, and flagged a second ticket that needed the same engine before both got built.

Data over opinion

A team assumed denials came from a stale ICD-10 code list. I traced them with SQL: 97% of non-billable-code denials came from retired codes still selectable in the SOAP note picker. The fix moved from a refresh schedule to the picker.
