01 / DuckDuckGoose

Designing trust for deepfake detection.

DuckDuckGoose, via NitrousAI trust and complianceUX Designer

DuckDuckGoose needed its marketing site to make deepfake detection feel accurate, understandable and trustworthy, for professionals in media, legal and enterprise security. I led UX and messaging strategy, shaping the IA and the concept.

  • Make the product feel transparent and explainable.
  • Build trust in a space full of fear and doubt.
  • Guide people to a demo request with less friction.
face swap
lip sync

Sweeps the file, finds the anomaly, names its type.

Research

A competitive scan of Hive, Reality Defender, Hyperverge, Sensity.ai and Microsoft Video Authenticator. Most were either too opaque or too technical. I also went through the model logic and demo videos with the product team.

The idea and the messaging

Deepfake detection as a radar scanner, like a metal detector sweeping a file for pixel-level anomalies, so people see not just that something is fake but what kind. The tone stayed calm and professional, built on safety, explainability and control, not AI hype. Wireframes echoed the scanner rhythm, with one hierarchy and CTAs placed in context.

Clarity and calm were more valuable than flashy AI terms.

Outcome

Potential clients could see how the AI works and why to trust it, especially in high-stakes work like news verification and legal evidence review.

02 / Trocco

Turning data workflows into human-centered experiences.

Trocco, via NitrousData engineering SaaSUX Strategist and Messaging Architect

Trocco manages modern data pipelines, and needed to connect with analysts and heads of data without losing technical accuracy. I led UX and messaging strategy to make a complex orchestration product approachable and conversion-ready.

Ingestion
Transformation
Output

Research

A competitive audit of Fivetran, Rivery, Airbyte and Hevo Data, plus early workshops and demos with the product team to understand how ingestion, transformation and automation really work behind the scenes.

The idea and the structure

An automated conveyor belt, a simple linear flow from ingestion to transformation to output. Each station became part of the site: the homepage built trust and clarity, Solutions segmented use cases by industry, and Product and Connector pages carried the technical depth. Messaging was mapped to analysts, decision makers and industry personas, and CTA paths were built around intent: demo, trial, partner contact. I delivered annotated wireframes for the homepage, solutions and connector pages.

We were not just designing pages. We were untangling how Trocco thinks about data.

Outcome

A clearer, more confident way to say what Trocco does. More than a metric, it gave the team a shared language.
